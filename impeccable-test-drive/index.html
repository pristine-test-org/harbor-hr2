Prepare for your consult

Your 45-minute call goes furthest when the advisor knows the situation before you dial in, so gather these three things first and keep them to hand.

Takes about 10 minutes

  • Who is involved

    Roles, tenure and the team each person sits in.

  • What has happened so far

    Dates, conversations and any written policy you relied on.

  • What you need to decide

    The one decision the advisor can help you make this week.
